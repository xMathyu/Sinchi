Hello, and thank you for the detailed review.

We have uploaded a new build (1.0.0, build 17) with the account deletion fix.
Below are the changes and the information you asked for.

GUIDELINE 5.1.1(v) - ACCOUNT DELETION

The app did offer account deletion, but a navigation bug made the option go
back to the home screen instead of opening it, and accounts just created with
Sign in with Apple or Google that do not belong to any gym yet did not show the
option at all. Both are fixed in this build:

- Where it is: tap the account icon at the top right to open "Mi cuenta" (My
  account), then "Eliminar mi cuenta" (Delete my account), right below "Cerrar
  sesion" (Sign out).
- A newly created account is deleted immediately after a confirmation step,
  together with its sign-in, and the app confirms "Tu cuenta se elimino" (Your
  account has been deleted).
- An account that is on a gym's roster, such as the demo account, is deleted
  from the same option. The gym has to keep its records of the payments it
  received for its accounting, so we complete that deletion within 30 days and
  the app shows that it is in progress. No phone call, email or other
  customer-service step is required.

The screen recording you requested, captured on a physical device, is attached
to this message and to the App Review Information.

GUIDELINE 2.1(b) - ARE THE ENTERPRISE SERVICES SOLD TO SINGLE USERS,
CONSUMERS, OR FOR FAMILY USE?

No. Sinchi is sold only to organizations: gyms and martial-arts schools that use
it to run their business (their student roster, checking students in at the
door, and recording the payments they receive at the counter). It is not sold
to single users, to consumers, or for family use. Students never pay Sinchi;
they pay their gym for in-person training.

GUIDELINE 3.1.1 - ACCOUNT REGISTRATION FOR BUSINESSES

We respectfully ask you to review this point under guideline 3.1.3(c),
Enterprise Services. The registration in the app creates an account for an
organization, a gym, and for its staff, who use Sinchi to manage the gym's
students:

- It asks for the gym's business name, its street address and, optionally, its
  RUC (the taxpayer number Peru issues to businesses).
- The plan is chosen by the size of the gym's student roster. A gym with up to
  10 students uses Sinchi free of charge. Larger gyms pay a monthly fee (S/ 149,
  S/ 299 or S/ 499 depending on the number of students) that we bill to the
  business outside the app; the business pays it by bank transfer. Nothing is
  purchased inside the app, and the first month of a paid plan is free.
- What the gym pays for is software to operate its physical business. It is not
  digital content consumed by an individual.

If you would still prefer that the registration of new gyms does not happen in
the iOS app, please let us know and we will remove it.

GUIDELINE 4.4 - WIDGETS

Sinchi does not include any widgets or other app extensions. We checked the
binary you reviewed (1.0.0, build 13): it contains no extensions. Could you tell
us which screen or element was identified as a widget, so that we can look at
it?
