Sinchi is a management app for gyms and martial-arts schools. The interface is in Spanish.

ANSWERS TO YOUR 2.1(b) QUESTIONS (submission 40e77983)
That submission was closed before we could reply, so our answers are here.

Sinchi sells no digital content, and no feature is unlocked by any payment. Two different things are paid for, neither consumed in the app:

1) A STUDENT'S GYM MEMBERSHIP. Bought by a person who trains at a specific gym. It buys the right to enter that physical location and train in person. Paid at the gym's counter in cash, Yape or bank transfer; staff record it by hand. In an upcoming version students will also be able to pay by card through Culqi, a Peruvian processor. The money goes to the gym. This is a service delivered outside the app, at a physical address, so per 3.1.3(e) it must not use In-App Purchase.

2) SINCHI'S OWN SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION, paid by the gym as a BUSINESS for the right to use Sinchi to run its roster. It is sold only on our website, sinchi.fit, and is never sold, offered, advertised or linked to inside this app. It is not sold to consumers or for family use.

What a student sees in the app is only the STATUS of a membership bought in person: their plan, sessions left this week, next payment date, whether they owe money, and a QR code the gym scans at the door.

The only digital material is a small library of technique videos each gym uploads for its own students. These are not sold, not priced, and not why anyone joins a gym; they are an incidental extra from a coach.

GUIDELINE 4.8
This build (13) adds Sign in with Apple next to Sign in with Google.

HOW TO SIGN IN
1. Open the app and tap "Entrar" (Sign in).
2. Type the demo credentials into "Correo" (email) and "Contrasena" (password).
3. Please do NOT use "Entrar con Google" or "Sign in with Apple" - the demo account works with email and password only.

WHAT THE DEMO ACCOUNT SHOWS
It owns a demo gym, "Sinchi Demo", with students, plans and payments loaded.
- Staff mode (default): student roster, door check-in by scanning a QR, manual payment records, plans, class schedule, routine videos.
- Member mode: tap the gear icon, then "Modo" > "Ver como alumno" (membership card, personal QR, schedule, routines).

CAMERA
Used only to scan students' QR codes at the door. If you prefer not to grant access, students can be checked in manually from the roster.
