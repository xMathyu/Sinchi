Sinchi is a management app for gyms and martial-arts schools. The interface is in Spanish.

DELETING AN ACCOUNT (5.1.1(v))
Tap the account icon at the top right to open "Mi cuenta" (My account), then "Eliminar mi cuenta" (Delete my account), below "Cerrar sesion" (Sign out). An account created with Sign in with Apple or Google that is not on any gym's roster yet is deleted immediately after confirming. An account on a gym's roster, like the demo account, is deleted within 30 days, because the gym keeps its payment records for accounting; the app shows the deletion in progress. No customer-service step is needed. A screen recording on a physical device is attached.

WHO PAYS FOR WHAT
Nothing is purchased inside the app, and no digital content is sold.

1) A STUDENT'S GYM MEMBERSHIP. Bought by a person who trains at a specific gym; it buys the right to enter that physical location and train in person. Paid at the gym's counter in cash, Yape or bank transfer, and recorded by the gym's staff. In an upcoming version students will also be able to pay by card through Culqi, a Peruvian processor, into the gym's OWN merchant account; Sinchi never holds that money and is not the merchant of record. This is a service delivered at a physical address, so per 3.1.3(e) it must not use In-App Purchase.

2) SINCHI'S SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION, an enterprise service (3.1.3(c)) sold only to organizations: gyms and martial-arts schools, for their staff to run their student roster. It is not sold to single users, consumers, or for family use, and it cannot be bought or registered for in the iOS app, which does not show its price or accept promo codes for it either. A gym owner only sees whether the gym's account is active or paused.

What a student sees is only the STATUS of a membership bought in person: plan, sessions left this week, next payment date, any amount owed, and a QR code the gym scans at the door. The only digital material is a small library of technique videos each gym uploads for its own students; they are not sold or priced.

ACCOUNT REGISTRATION FOR BUSINESSES (3.1.1)
Removed, as requested. The iOS app no longer lets anyone register a gym or open an additional location: the option is gone from the sign-in screen, from "Mi cuenta" (My account) and from the gym directory, and the registration screen cannot be opened by link either. The only accounts that can still be created in the iOS app are personal ones, for students and staff of a gym that already uses Sinchi.

WIDGETS (4.4)
The app has no widgets or other app extensions.

HOW TO SIGN IN
1. Open the app and tap "Entrar" (Sign in).
2. Type the demo credentials into "Correo" (email) and "Contrasena" (password).
3. Please do NOT use the Google or Apple buttons below: the demo account works with email and password only.

WHAT THE DEMO ACCOUNT SHOWS
It owns a demo gym, "Sinchi Demo", with students, plans and payments loaded.
- Staff mode (default): student roster, door check-in by scanning a QR, manual payment records, plans, class schedule, routine videos.
- Member mode: tap the account icon, then "Modo" > "Ver como alumno" (membership card, personal QR, schedule, routines).

CAMERA
Used only to scan students' QR codes at the door. If you prefer not to grant access, students can be checked in manually from the roster.
