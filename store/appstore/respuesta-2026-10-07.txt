Thank you for the clarification. As requested under Guideline 3.1.1, we have removed the account registration feature for businesses from the iOS app.

In the new build, nobody can register a gym or open an additional gym location from the iOS app: the option is gone from the sign-in screen, from "Mi cuenta" (My account) and from the gym directory, and the registration screen no longer opens from a link either. The only accounts that can still be created in the iOS app are personal ones, for students and staff of a gym that already uses Sinchi.

The demo account in App Review Information is unchanged and still owns the demo gym, so both the staff and the student sides can be reviewed with it.
