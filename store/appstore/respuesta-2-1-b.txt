Hello, and thank you for the review.

We have uploaded a new build (1.0.0, build 13) that adds Sign in with Apple to
address guideline 4.8. Below are the answers to your questions about our
business model.

In short: Sinchi sells no digital content. Two different things are paid for,
and neither is consumed inside the app. One is in-person training at a physical
gym. The other is a business software subscription that gym owners buy from us
on our website.

1. WHO ARE THE USERS THAT WILL USE THE PAID SUBSCRIPTIONS IN THE APP?

Two different things are called a "subscription" in our product, and they are
paid by different people:

a) A STUDENT'S GYM MEMBERSHIP. The user is a person who trains at a specific
   gym or martial-arts school. What the membership buys is the right to walk
   into that physical location and train there in person.

b) SINCHI'S OWN SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION. The customer is the gym as a business,
   and what it buys is the right to use Sinchi to run its student roster. This
   is sold to businesses, never to consumers.

2. WHERE CAN USERS PURCHASE THE SUBSCRIPTIONS THAT CAN BE ACCESSED IN THE APP?

a) Gym memberships are purchased at the gym, in person. Today they are paid in
   cash, by Yape (a Peruvian bank-transfer app), or by bank transfer, and a
   staff member records the payment by hand in Sinchi. In an upcoming version
   we intend to also let students pay by card through Culqi, a Peruvian payment
   processor. In every case the money goes to the gym, for in-person training at
   the gym's physical location. Sinchi is not the seller of that service.

b) Sinchi's software subscription is purchased by gym owners on our website,
   sinchi.fit, and billed through Culqi. It is not sold, offered, advertised, or
   linked to anywhere inside the iOS app.

3. WHAT SPECIFIC TYPES OF PREVIOUSLY PURCHASED SUBSCRIPTIONS CAN A USER ACCESS
   IN THE APP?

None that consist of digital content. A student sees the STATUS of the physical
gym membership they purchased in person:

- which plan they are on (for example, "2 sessions per week"),
- how many sessions they have left this week,
- the date of their next payment and whether they currently owe money,
- a QR code that the gym scans to let them through the door.

This is a record of a service that is delivered entirely outside the app, at a
physical address. Per guideline 3.1.3(e), services consumed outside the app must
use purchase methods other than In-App Purchase, which is why we do not use it.

4. WHAT PAID CONTENT, SUBSCRIPTIONS, OR FEATURES ARE UNLOCKED WITHIN THE APP
   THAT DO NOT USE IN-APP PURCHASE?

No digital content is sold, and none is unlocked by a payment made to us.

The gym membership pays for physical access to a gym. The app's role is to show
whether that membership is current and to open the door with a QR code.

The only digital material in the app is a small library of technique videos that
each gym uploads for its own students (warm-ups, drills, technique reviews).
These are not sold, are not priced, and are not part of what a student is buying:
they are an incidental extra a coach shares with people who already train at the
gym in person, in the same way a coach might send a clip over a messaging app. No
student joins a gym to obtain them, and no gym advertises them as a reason to
pay.

Every feature of the iOS app is available at no charge to anyone who signs in.

5. ARE THE ENTERPRISE SERVICES IN YOUR APP SOLD TO SINGLE USERS, CONSUMERS, OR
   FOR FAMILY USE?

They are sold to businesses. Our customers are the owners of gyms and
martial-arts schools, who use Sinchi to operate a physical business: keeping a
roster, checking students in at the door, and recording payments received at the
counter. Sinchi is not sold to single consumers and not for family use.

Students never pay Sinchi anything. They pay their gym, for in-person training.

We are happy to provide a video walkthrough of the full flow, or any other
information that would help.
