Hello, and thank you for the review.

Sinchi does not sell, unlock, or provide access to any paid digital content,
subscriptions, or features. The app is free, contains no In-App Purchases, and
processes no payments of any kind. Below are answers to each question.

1. WHO ARE THE USERS THAT WILL USE THE PAID SUBSCRIPTIONS IN THE APP?

There are no subscriptions sold or consumed in the app. The word "plan" or
"membership" in Sinchi refers to a PHYSICAL GYM MEMBERSHIP: the right to walk
into a specific gym or martial-arts school and train there in person.

Two kinds of people use the app, and neither buys anything through it:
- Gym owners and front-desk staff, who use Sinchi to keep their student roster,
  check students in at the door, and write down payments they received in cash
  at the counter.
- Students of those gyms, who use Sinchi to see the status of the membership
  they already bought in person, and to show a QR code at the door.

2. WHERE CAN USERS PURCHASE THE SUBSCRIPTIONS THAT CAN BE ACCESSED IN THE APP?

Only in person, at the gym's front desk. In Peru these are paid in cash, by Yape
(a local bank transfer app), or by bank transfer, directly to the gym. A staff
member then records that payment by hand in Sinchi.

There is no way to buy anything inside Sinchi, and no payment processor of any
kind is integrated. Sinchi never touches the money: the gym receives it directly
and Sinchi only stores a record that it was received.

3. WHAT SPECIFIC TYPES OF PREVIOUSLY PURCHASED SUBSCRIPTIONS CAN A USER ACCESS
   IN THE APP?

None that are digital. What a student sees is the STATUS of a physical gym
membership purchased in person:
- which plan they are on (for example, "2 sessions per week"),
- how many sessions they have left this week,
- the date of their next payment and whether they currently owe money,
- a QR code that the gym scans to let them through the door.

This is a record of a service consumed entirely outside the app, in a physical
location. Per guideline 3.1.3(e), services consumed outside the app must not use
In-App Purchase, which is why Sinchi does not.

4. WHAT PAID CONTENT, SUBSCRIPTIONS, OR FEATURES ARE UNLOCKED WITHIN THE APP
   THAT DO NOT USE IN-APP PURCHASE?

None. Every feature of the app is available for free to anyone who signs in.

The only content in the app is a small library of technique videos that each gym
uploads for its own students (warm-ups, drills, technique reviews). These are not
sold, separately or as part of anything: they are a free extra the gym publishes
for people who already train there in person, in the same way a coach might send
a video over a messaging app. Nothing in the app is placed behind a payment.

5. ARE THE ENTERPRISE SERVICES IN YOUR APP SOLD TO SINGLE USERS, CONSUMERS, OR
   FOR FAMILY USE?

Sinchi is a business tool for gym owners, used to run a physical business. It is
currently offered to gyms free of charge, and there is no paid tier inside the
app. It is not sold to consumers or for family use, and it is not sold through
the app at all.

other information you need.
